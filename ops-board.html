Import your handover PDF
Drop the PDF you use today (“Assignments for handover”). Jobs, ETA/ETD, squawks, crew, WOA stage and the whole handover history are read, and every fictitious job is replaced. The type (RTS or Assist) is guessed from the squawks, and jobs whose aircraft left more than 2 days ago go to “Waiting for parts”: both can be corrected with Edit.
